Privacy Policy – Scribble Slayer

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Publisher: Selo Corp · Contact: selocorp@outlook.com

This policy explains what information the mobile game Scribble Slayer (the "App"), published by Selo Corp ("we", "us"), handles and why.

1. Information we do not collect

The App has no accounts, no login and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that receive data from the App.

2. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (gold, levels, weapons, wings, settings such as language and sound) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its services, the SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device and advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID, App set ID)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address (may be used to estimate approximate location)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Product interactions (e.g. app launches, ad taps, video views)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostic information (e.g. performance and crash data of the SDK)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Rewarded videos are always optional and are only shown when you tap a button such as "Revive", "2X Gold" or "Free Chest".

4. Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Ads in the App are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rating "PG").

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and security

We do not hold any personal data on our side. Locally stored progress stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, "KVKK"), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, but you can always contact us at selocorp@outlook.com and we will help.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Selo Corp – selocorp@outlook.com
